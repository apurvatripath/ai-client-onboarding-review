Turn a new client brief into a review-ready handoff.

Collect the essential project details and one supporting document. The workflow validates the intake, extracts structured information and prepares missing-item follow-up for a person to approve.

    Validate the intake

    Required details, URL, date, document type and size are checked first.

  2. 02

    Extract agreed fields and identify anything missing or uncertain.

  3. 03

    The review is recorded and any follow-up remains a draft until approved.
